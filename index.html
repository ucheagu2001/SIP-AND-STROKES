Our Mobile Pop-Up & Community Mission

Art Anywhere: Mobile Workshops, Charitable Events & Youth Programs

Sip & Strokes Canada is a 100% mobile paint studio. Instead of requiring guests to visit a static physical building, we specialize in bringing top-tier art experiences directly to where people naturally gather—hotel lounges, rooftop patios, community halls, corporate offices, and private backyard settings.

Beyond commercial gatherings, we are deeply committed to social impact. We regularly organize charitable and sponsored events to raise funds and awareness for local causes, while also offering dedicated subsidized youth summer classes for ages 6 to 16. We provide a complete pop-up setup with portable easels, protective coverings, canvases, brushes, and paints so every participant can enjoy a seamless, inspiring experience.

Our Story & Mobile Philosophy

Sip & Strokes Canada was established with a singular mission: to bring art, relaxation, and genuine human connection directly into communities, hotels, and vibrant local spaces. By removing the boundaries of a traditional static brick-and-mortar studio, we take creative experiences anywhere people gather.

From fine art commissions and youth summer programs (ages 6–16) to charitable fundraising and hotel activations, our team brings color, joy, and collaborative energy to every venue we partner with.

The Creator Behind The Work

Principal Artist & Studio Director

Visual Arts, Upcycling & Mobile Events

"My creative work is rooted in the belief that discarded materials and natural elements possess deep historical resonance. By blending sustainable upcycling with modern visual composition, I aim to bridge environmental mindfulness with striking fine art."

Biography & Creative Philosophy

As the founder and lead creator of Sip & Strokes Canada, my artistic journey spans mobile event direction, tactile sculpture, textile design, and studio portraiture. I collaborate with local builders, community spaces, and environmental restoration projects to source reclaimed materials that would otherwise be discarded.

Every piece in my collection is handmade through a meticulous process of cleaning, shaping, structuring, and sealing with eco-conscious finishes. Whether crafting interactive pop-up paint workshops, teaching youth summer classes, or building sculptural wall panels, my goal is to make art accessible, sustainable, and deeply engaging.
