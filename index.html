Our Mobile Pop-Up & Community Mission

Art Anywhere: Mobile Workshops, Charitable Events & Youth Programs

Sip & Strokes Canada is a 100% mobile paint studio. Instead of requiring guests to visit a static physical building, we specialize in bringing top-tier art experiences directly to where people naturally gather—hotel lounges, rooftop patios, community halls, corporate offices, and private backyard settings.

Beyond commercial gatherings, we are deeply committed to social impact. We regularly organize charitable and sponsored events to raise funds and awareness for local causes, while also offering dedicated subsidized youth summer classes for ages 6 to 16. We provide a complete pop-up setup with portable easels, protective coverings, canvases, brushes, and paints so every participant can enjoy a seamless, inspiring experience.

About Us

Our Vision & Purpose

Transforming Materials, Giving Back & Building Community Together

Sip & Strokes Canada was established with a singular mission: to bring art, relaxation, and genuine human connection directly into communities, hotels, and vibrant local spaces. By removing the boundaries of a traditional static brick-and-mortar studio, we take creative experiences anywhere people gather.

At the heart of our studio practice is a profound commitment to upcycle art and sustainability. We believe that discarded objects—reclaimed urban timber, scrap metals, and everyday materials—hold deep narratives waiting to be reimagined into enduring fine art and architectural wall reliefs.

More than just an art studio, we believe in radical community impact. A dedicated portion of proceeds from our upcycled art sales and mobile workshops directly funds our subsidized youth summer art classes (ages 6 to 16) and local community charitable fundraisers.

Be Part of the Vision: When you acquire an original upcycled piece, book a mobile sip-and-paint night, or partner with us as a venue host, you aren't just engaging with art—you are actively helping us mentor the next generation of creators, reduce environmental waste, and keep community-driven creativity alive across Canada.

Community Action & Studio Presence

Bringing People Together Through Creativity

Every pop-up event and community gathering we host is designed to break down barriers and make artistic expression welcoming to everyone. From hotel lounge activations to hands-on youth workshops, our mobile setup provides all the guidance, supplies, and positive energy needed for a memorable experience.

We invite you to join our growing network of community partners, art collectors, and participants shaping a more vibrant, sustainable cultural landscape across Canada.

Upcycle / Visual Artist

Aik Agu

Visual Arts, Upcycling & Cultural Storytelling

"My work is ultimately about identity, memory, transformation, and the enduring power of culture."

Biography & Artistic Practice

Aik Agu is a Nigerian-Canadian visual artist whose multidisciplinary practice explores African identity, cultural memory, spirituality, and symbolism. His work spans painting, sculpture, wood-panel art, mixed media, and upcycled materials, transforming discarded and everyday objects into meaningful works of art.

Deeply influenced by his Nigerian heritage, Agu incorporates elements of Nsibidi and Uli into contemporary compositions, using traditional visual languages to explore ancestry, identity, spirituality, and the connection between past and present.

With formal training in Art and Design, specializing in Sculpture, Agu approaches his practice as both an artist and a cultural storyteller. His use of upcycled materials reflects his belief in transformation—that materials, stories, and cultural knowledge that might otherwise be forgotten can be given new life and meaning.

As a Nigerian-Canadian artist, Agu's work bridges African heritage and contemporary global experiences. Through his art, he seeks to preserve cultural memory while reinterpreting it for a new generation and an international audience.

Photographer's Biography & Approach

With over a decade of dedicated professional experience immersed in the visual arts, our lead photographer brings a meticulous painterly eye to every single frame. Drawing heavy inspiration from classic chiaroscuro lighting and contemporary editorial aesthetics, each session is meticulously structured to highlight unique individual character, professional brand identity, and raw human emotion.

Whether working inside our controlled studio environment with precision-shaped lighting modifiers or capturing candid photojournalism at vibrant mobile events across Canada, the creative process is always collaborative, relaxed, and deeply empowering. We believe that true photographic magic happens when subjects feel completely at ease, allowing their genuine personalities to radiate naturally through the lens.

From striking executive branding headshots and cinematic portraiture to detailed product stills and high-energy event coverage, every project receives unwavering dedication to color grading, composition, and visual storytelling excellence.
